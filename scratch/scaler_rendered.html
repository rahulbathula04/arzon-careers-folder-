About this Masterclass

The AI PM role is becoming the default expectation at every product-led company. From early-stage startups to Google and Microsoft, teams are actively looking for PMs who can speak the language of AI: evaluate models, shape prompts, and build products that actually use intelligence well. 

In this free masterclass by Amar Srivastava, CEO - Online Business, Scaler, on Tuesday, 8th September, 7:30 – 10:00 PM, you’ll learn exactly what it takes to become an AI PM that companies are looking to hire these days.

Register Now!

What you will gain from this Masterclass
  • The AI PM salary premium — why companies pay 40-60% more
  • Skills gap audit: what you already have vs. what's missing
  • The 3 AI concepts every PM must own (model evaluation, prompt design, RAG pipelines — explained simply)
  • How to write AI PRDs that engineers respect
  • Reframing your current PM experience as AI-relevant (you're closer than you think)
  • Hiring insider secrets: what Google, Microsoft, and top startups screen for
  • How PGP in Business and AI can help!

Who is this Event For?

This masterclass is especially suited for:

  • Professionals in product, operations, marketing, finance, strategy, and general management
  • Founders and aspiring entrepreneurs navigating AI-led business change
  • Decision-makers and future leaders, not just task executors
  • Professionals who see AI becoming part of their role and want clarity on how to use it effectively

About Scaler's Online PGP in Business & AI

Scaler’s Online Post Graduate Program in Business & AI is designed for professionals who want to stay relevant and competitive in an AI-driven business environment.

The program combines:

  • Core business fundamentals (strategy, marketing, finance, operations, leadership)
  • Applied AI understanding tailored for business professionals
  • Real-world case studies, hands-on projects, and industry-relevant applications

Rather than treating AI as a separate technical subject, the program integrates AI across business decision-making, helping professionals learn how to:

  • Use AI to solve business problems
  • Make better, faster, data-backed decisions
  • Lead teams and initiatives in AI-enabled organisations

This session offers a preview of the thinking, frameworks, and approach you will experience inside the Scaler PGP — helping you evaluate whether the program aligns with your career goals.
