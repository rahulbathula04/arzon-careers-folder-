# 70 / 20 / 10 content audit — public website

## Framework recap

Per [Smart Insights](https://www.smartinsights.com/marketing-planning/marketing-models/using-the-702010-rule-in-marketing/) and the Coca‑Cola content rule:

- **70 % — Value / desire-building.** Original content that helps the
  reader, builds the dream outcome, teaches the domain. No ask.
- **20 % — Social proof / others' voices.** Learner stories, recruiter
  quotes, JD screenshots, mentor bios, press, partner logos.
- **10 % — Direct selling.** Apply, price, urgency, seat-reservation,
  coupon, deadline.

Ratio is measured by **vertical pixel share** on desktop (1280 × 800).
Target is 70 / 20 / 10 ± 5 ppt. Pages may exceed 10 on the selling band
only when they are _action pages_ (e.g. `/apply`).

## Two failure modes I'm explicitly hunting

1. **CTA carpet bombing** — every section ends with "Apply". Cap at
   three explicit Apply blocks per page.
2. **"Us talking about us" inflation** — copy reads as social proof but
   is actually our own marketing voice. Down-weight or replace with a
   real quote / screenshot.

- No rebalance work unless a specific block is egregious.
